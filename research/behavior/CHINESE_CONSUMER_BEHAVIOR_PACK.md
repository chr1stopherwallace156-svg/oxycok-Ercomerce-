---

# Psychological Construct Rule

Constructs such as:

- trust;
- immersion;
- arousal;
- perceived value;
- cognitive load;
- perceived intrusiveness;

are latent variables.

They are not directly observable merely because an AI says they exist.

Measurement requires declared proxies or validated instruments.

---

# Manipulation Boundary

Behavioral research may identify persuasive mechanisms.

That does not automatically authorize aggressive use.

Every intervention should be classified:

ASSISTIVE
PERSUASIVE
HIGH_PRESSURE
MANIPULATIVE
PROHIBITED

Classification must consider:

- user control;
- transparency;
- reversibility;
- vulnerability;
- frequency;
- urgency;
- economic consequence.

---

# Impulse-Purchase Guardrail

If an intervention is designed to increase impulse purchasing, monitor:

PRIMARY:
- conversion;
- revenue/session.

GUARDRAILS:
- refunds;
- returns;
- cancellation;
- customer complaints;
- unsubscribe;
- repeat purchase;
- satisfaction where measurable.

Short-term conversion alone is insufficient.

---

# Trust as a Business Metric

Recommendation quality should eventually track:

- explanation interaction;
- personalization opt-out;
- recommendation dismissals;
- repeated use;
- recommendation CTR;
- conversion;
- post-purchase outcomes.

Trust should not be reduced to one synthetic score unless the score is explicitly validated.
